# Transport data attribution

Bus Arrival v3 and Bus Stops data are provided by Singapore's Land Transport Authority (LTA) through [DataMall](https://datamall.lta.gov.sg/content/datamall/en.html), under the [Singapore Open Data Licence v1.0](https://datamall.lta.gov.sg/content/datamall/en/SingaporeOpenDataLicence.html). This is independent software and is not an official or endorsed LTA product. Live arrival cards show retrieval dates/times; sample timings are synthetic and labelled as such. The stop directory may be cached locally.

Users must obtain and protect their own DataMall access key and comply with the [LTA API Terms of Service](https://datamall.lta.gov.sg/content/datamall/en/api-terms-of-service.html), including applicable access limits and additional API-specific requirements. Do not share keys, evade limits or misrepresent client identity. Disconnect and inform LTA immediately if suspicious activity or a vulnerability is detected in systems interfacing with LTA. Data and estimates may be inaccurate, delayed or unavailable. For broader data rights see [data.gov.sg](https://data.gov.sg/).

BAY65's MIT licence covers its original code only, not LTA data, trademarks, third-party works or API access.
