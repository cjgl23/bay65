Home is your next stop.

An Android TV launcher with live Singapore bus arrivals, your favourite apps and your own photos on one screen.

When is my next bus?

The 36 at your stop arrives in 4 minutes with seats available. The one after is about 13 minutes behind it.

Ask without leaving the show.

The optional BAY65 Assistant is a floating orb that answers questions about your buses and your apps, using the TV's own speech. Bring a key from Google Gemini, Groq or OpenRouter, or point it at your own Ollama server.

It stays off until you set it up.

Everywhere Mode puts it one button away, over any app.

  1. Connect a provider. In Settings, choose BAY65 Assistant, enter your key and pick Save and test.
  2. Turn on Everywhere Mode. Allow BAY65 to display over other apps.
  3. Choose a shortcut. Set it to Menu, then enable BAY65 remote shortcut in Accessibility.
  4. Ask from anywhere. Press Menu over another app. Press Back to return to it.

Your keys and photos stay on your TV.

  • Encrypted keys

    Your LTA and assistant keys are encrypted with Android Keystore. None are bundled with the app.

  • Private photos

    Imported photos are copied to app-private storage and shown only on your screen.

  • No tracking

    No BAY65 accounts, analytics, advertising or cloud storage. Backup extraction is turned off.

  • Microphone on request

    The assistant listens only after you open it and ask. It never reads other apps' screens.

Build it from source.

BAY65 is shared as MIT-licensed source, without a public APK. You need JDK 17 or later and Android SDK platform 36 for TVs running Android 8.0 and up.
